Sobre mi

Joel López, also known as JOTA, is a designer and art director focused on brand communication and digital media.

He approaches every project with a strategic mindset, building cohesive visual systems that elevate market positioning and message clarity.

He has worked in marketing agencies and growth/paid media teams both nationally (Chile) and internationally, leading multidisciplinary teams to drive measurable and consistent results.
